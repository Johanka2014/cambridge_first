2. Listening Part 3

SB p.31

1 Prepare for Listening

Exam Strategy: The speakers will not use exactly the same words as the statements A-H. Read the statements below and underline the key ideas. Then, try to paraphrase them (write the same idea in different words) before you listen.

Statement A

I didn't enjoy it much at first.

Suggestion: "It wasn't fun at the start" / "I didn't like it initially"

Statement B

I didn't mind the discomfort.

Suggestion: "The bad conditions didn't bother me" / "It was uncomfortable but okay"

Statement C

I got to know lots of people.

Suggestion: "I made many new friends" / "I met a lot of new contacts"

Statement D

I'd done something similar before.

Suggestion: "It wasn't my first time" / "I had experience with this"

Statement E

I wanted a low-cost holiday.

Suggestion: "I wanted a cheap trip" / "I was on a budget"

Statement F

I didn't do much during the day.

Suggestion: "I was lazy in the daytime" / "We only went out at night"

Statement G

I wasn't in as much danger as imagined.

Suggestion: "It was safer than I thought" / "People worried but it was fine"

Statement H

I went on the trip as a break from my parents.

Suggestion: "I wanted to get away from my mum and dad"

  • Journey: The process of travelling from A to B.
